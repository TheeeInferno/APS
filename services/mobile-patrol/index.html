Service Overview

Active property coverage without a stationary officer.

Mobile patrol provides an active, visible presence across your property on a schedule designed around its needs. It can be an effective option for sites that need regular oversight without fixed on-site coverage around the clock.

APS works with each client to establish patrol expectations and a reporting process.

Service Detail

What mobile patrol can include.

Patrol operations combine consistent checks with documentation and client communication.

Scheduled checks

Property visits planned around the service agreement and site needs.

Randomized patrols

Variable patrol timing to support a visible deterrent presence.

Property inspections

Observation of agreed access points, common areas, and site conditions.

Documented activity

Clear incident and observation reporting for client follow-up.
